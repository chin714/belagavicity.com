Belagavi, also known as Belgaum, sits where Karnataka, Maharashtra and Goa meet. You can hear three languages at a single bus stop here, and an 800 year old fort stands a short drive from a modern aerospace park. Whether you're moving here, visiting, studying or starting a business, this guide covers what you need to know.

## Location and how to get here

Belagavi (formerly Belgaum, renamed in 2014) is in northwest Karnataka, in the foothills of the Western Ghats. The city is about 750 metres above sea level, which helps keep it cooler than most of the Deccan plateau.

- **Bengaluru:** about 500 km to the southeast
- **Mumbai:** about 500 km to the north
- **Goa (Panaji):** about 150 km to the west
- **Kolhapur:** about 110 km to the north
- **Hubballi Dharwad:** about 100 km to the southeast

Belagavi Airport at Sambra has direct flights to several major Indian cities. The city is also on the main rail line between Bengaluru and Pune, and National Highway 48 runs past it.

## A city with deep history

Belgaum Fort was built in the early 13th century, and inside it stands Kamal Basti, a Jain temple known for its carved lotus ceiling. Nearby Kittur is the home of Rani Chennamma, one of the first rulers in India to take up arms against the British East India Company.

In 1924 Belagavi hosted the session of the Indian National Congress presided over by Mahatma Gandhi. It was the only Congress session he ever presided over. Today the city is home to the Suvarna Vidhana Soudha, where the Karnataka Legislature holds its winter session. That has earned Belagavi the informal title of the state's second capital.

## Culture and language

Belagavi is truly bilingual. Kannada and Marathi are both spoken widely, and you'll also hear Konkani, Urdu and Hindi. You can see this mix in its festivals:

- **Ganeshotsav** is celebrated on a grand scale, with large public pandals across the city.
- **Dasara, Diwali, Eid, Christmas and Mahavir Jayanti** are all celebrated, reflecting the city's diverse communities.
- **Rajyotsava** on 1 November is marked with processions and cultural programmes.

**Food:** No trip is complete without **Belgaum kunda**, a rich caramelised milk sweet the city is famous for. Look out for **Gokak karadantu**, from the nearby town of Gokak, along with north Karnataka staples like jolada rotti, ennegayi and spicy misal.

The city also has a strong military presence. It is home to the Maratha Light Infantry Regimental Centre, an Air Force station and army training establishments, and the green, orderly cantonment area is one of the most pleasant parts of town.

## Weather

Belagavi has one of the most comfortable climates in Karnataka.

| Season | Months | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Summer | March to May | Warm days, usually 30°C to 35°C, with cool evenings |
| Monsoon | June to September | Heavy, steady rain and green hills, with waterfalls nearby |
| Winter | November to February | Pleasant days and cool nights, sometimes dropping to about 12°C |

The monsoon is the best time to visit nearby spots like Gokak Falls, Amboli and the Dandeli forests.

## Education

Belagavi is a major education hub for north Karnataka and the bordering regions:

- **Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU)** has its headquarters here. It oversees a large number of engineering colleges across Karnataka.
- **KLE Academy of Higher Education and Research** runs Jawaharlal Nehru Medical College and a large teaching hospital.
- **Belagavi Institute of Medical Sciences (BIMS)** is a government medical college.
- **Rani Channamma University** offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in many subjects.
- **Gogte Institute of Technology** and several other engineering, dental, pharmacy, law and management colleges are also here.

Students come from across Karnataka, Maharashtra and Goa, which keeps the city young and lively.

## Industries

Belagavi has a strong and growing industrial base.

- **Foundry and casting:** Industrial areas like Udyambag, Mache and Kakati have a large number of foundries supplying castings to automotive and engineering companies in India and abroad.
- **Aerospace and precision engineering:** The aerospace SEZ at Hattargi makes components for global aircraft manufacturers.
- **Hydraulics and machine tools:** Many small and medium units make hydraulic parts, pumps and machined components.
- **Sugar and agriculture:** Belagavi district is one of the largest sugarcane growing regions in Karnataka and has many sugar factories. Other important crops are rice, jowar, soybean and vegetables.

## Business in Belagavi

The local business scene is varied and very active:

- **Retail and wholesale** markets around Khade Bazar, Ganpat Galli and Shahapur
- **Healthcare,** with large multispeciality hospitals and many clinics and diagnostic centres
- **Hospitality,** with hotels and restaurants serving business travellers and Goa bound tourists
- **Real estate and construction,** growing steadily as the city expands
- **Home services** like electricians, plumbers, carpenters and repair shops

Belagavi was also selected under the Smart Cities Mission, which has brought investment into roads, public spaces and civic services.

## Jobs and careers

Belagavi offers a wide range of jobs:

- **Manufacturing and engineering:** foundries, aerospace units and machine shops hire engineers, technicians and skilled workers.
- **Education and healthcare:** universities, colleges and hospitals are among the city's biggest employers.
- **Defence:** the military establishments create civilian jobs too.
- **Retail, hospitality and services:** these are growing with the city's population.
- **IT and remote work:** with a lower cost of living than the metros, more professionals are choosing to work remotely from Belagavi.

## Why people love living here

Clean air, a mild climate, affordable living, good schools and hospitals, and Goa just a few hours away. Belagavi offers the facilities of a growing city while keeping the calm of a smaller town.
